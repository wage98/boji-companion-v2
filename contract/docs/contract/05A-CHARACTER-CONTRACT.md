# 05A — Character Contract

Status: **DECISION-FROZEN DIRECTION / FINAL ASSET PENDING**

## BOJI-CHAR-001 — Identity

The official Boji character MUST be an **original anime-style sweet adult female training companion**.

She is a training companion first, not an AI girlfriend, not a coach authority, and not a derivative of a known anime/game character.

## BOJI-CHAR-002 — Adult visual identity

The character MUST read clearly as an adult woman.

MUST NOT:
- use childlike body proportions;
- use strongly juvenile facial/styling cues;
- be presented as underage.

## BOJI-CHAR-003 — Personality

Core presentation:
- sweet;
- warm;
- energetic;
- gentle;
- intelligent;
- supportive;
- light athletic confidence.

She should feel like a long-term training companion rather than a commanding trainer.

## BOJI-CHAR-004 — Visual language

Preferred direction:
- modern Japanese-inspired anime rendering;
- medium/long hair, with training-friendly ponytail variation allowed;
- deep mint / warm orange / cream / limited lilac accents;
- simple athletic outerwear / training jacket;
- recognizable Boji `B` brand element or equivalent original mark;
- strong silhouette at small size.

The production default MUST NOT drift into excessive sexualization.

## BOJI-CHAR-005 — Product adaptability

The same character system MUST support:
- home hero portrait;
- chat/Boji center portrait;
- PWA/app icon simplification;
- chibi desktop-pet form;
- emotion portraits;
- lightweight motion/animation states.

## BOJI-CHAR-006 — Required state family

At minimum design for:
- neutral/warm;
- happy;
- encourage;
- focused;
- proud/PR;
- concerned;
- tired;
- sleep.

Motion/event candidates:
- idle/blink;
- set complete;
- rest;
- workout complete;
- PR celebration;
- stretch;
- sleep.

These are presentation states only. They MUST be triggered from verified product state/events and MUST NOT create health/training facts.

## BOJI-CHAR-007 — Originality / provenance

Final production assets MUST:
- be original;
- have recorded provenance;
- avoid use of third-party official character assets;
- avoid distinctive copied combinations of existing character hairstyle, costume, symbols, silhouette, and accessories.

Known old third-party/derivative assets are not valid production branding.

## BOJI-CHAR-008 — Photo Pet coexistence

Official Boji MUST NOT replace the user's Photo Pet capability.

Both coexist:
- official Boji brand companion;
- optional user-local Photo Pet personalization.

## Asset status

Current generated anime boards are **APPROVED CANDIDATE DIRECTION ONLY**.

They are not:
- final production assets;
- exact UI authority;
- proof of asset originality/licensing;
- frozen app icons.

A later Character Asset Freeze must review actual production files.
